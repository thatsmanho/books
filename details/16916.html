This book is about twelve women who live in Tin Shui Wai. Typical of the fair sex in the local community, they are faced with problems related to employment, transport, family, marriage, children, mental health and chronic diseases. Although the district may or may not have featured in their lives in the past, it does bond them together now as the place where they have set up home.
