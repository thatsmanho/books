Set in Russia just after the communist revolution of 1917, Ayn Rand's first novel is the story of three idealistic young people who struggle to retain their vision of life against a totalitarian system.

We The Living is also movie, produced in Italy 1942. The movie stars Alida Valli, Fosco Giachetti, and Rossano Brazzi. Of the movie, Ayn Rand said, "The cast, direction and production are excellent" and "the performance of the girl in the starring part is magnificent."
